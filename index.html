Prophetic Traditions

Sunnahs & Etiquette of Nikah

"Marriage is part of my Sunnah, and whoever does not follow my Sunnah has nothing to do with me." — Sunan Ibn Majah

01

Honoring the Invite

Accepting a wedding invitation and attending the gathering with joy and pure intentions is an emphasized Sunnah that strengthens brotherhood and family ties.

02

Blessing the Couple

Greet the newlyweds with the Prophetic supplication: "Barakallahu lakuma wa baraka 'alaykuma..." praying for lasting harmony and righteousness.

03

Sacred Time (After Asar)

Conducting the Nikah after Asar Namaz (4:30 PM) gathers the angels of mercy and fills the beginning of their marital life with barakah.

04

The Reception Feast

Partaking in the wedding meal after the Nikah and praying for the hosts ("Allahumma barik lahum fima razaqtahum") completes the Sunnah of celebration.
